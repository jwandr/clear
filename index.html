A gentle reminder

You're not trying to win at sobriety. You're finding out what helps you feel better.

An urge is a moment, not an instruction.

Log what is happening without judging yourself. You don't have to make the feeling disappear to respond differently.

Let's soften the edges

Choose what is behind the urge, then log it for a gentle suggestion tailored to the moment.

Try the next five minutes

  • Change rooms or step outside briefly.
  • Pour a drink you actually like.
  • Relax your jaw and shoulders; take a few slow breaths.
  • Give yourself permission to do nothing productive.

An urge isn't a failure. If stopping alcohol causes shaking, sweating, nausea or significant anxiety, seek medical advice; withdrawal can be dangerous.
